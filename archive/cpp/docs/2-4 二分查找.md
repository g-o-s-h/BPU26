
### 二分查找 binary search

```

0 1 2 3  4  5  6   7
1 5 6 8 24 72 73 100

key = 6

a[3] -> 8 > 6

left = 0, right = 2 -> mid = 1
a[1] -> 5 < 6

left = 2, right = 2 -> mid = 2
a[2] -> 6 = 6
return 2


key = 80

a[6] -> 73 < 80

left = 7, right = 6
left > right
```
